## Methodological Approach

Every investigation follows a structured process:

1. **Scope Definition** – What is included and what is excluded
2. **Passive Collection** – Public sources only, no active interaction
3. **Correlation & Analysis** – Linking data from different sources
4. **Risk Assessment** – Confidence level and impact evaluation
5. **Clear Reporting** – Executive summary + technical details + limitations

Main areas of focus:
- Corporate Footprint & Asset Discovery
- Shadow IT Identification
- Brand Protection & Impersonation
- Geolocation and Temporal Analysis
- People & Company Due Diligence
